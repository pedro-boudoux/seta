TRADEMARKS AND SYMBOLS VOL. 2 — EXTRACTED LOGOS
================================================

Source: "Trademarks and Symbols Vol. 2: Symbolical Designs"
(scanned PDF). Every numbered plate logo, traced to clean
standalone SVG (black on transparent, tight viewBox).

Contents
--------
logo-NNN.svg   1413 logos named by their printed plate number (91-1503)
p27_05.svg     one plate mark whose tiny printed number is illegible
p80_03.svg     one mark on the final plate with no printed number
editorial/     86 example marks from the essay pages (PDF pages 9-12),
               named pNN_XX (page + reading order)
cover/         the cover collage as a single plate (the cover marks
               touch each other in print and cannot be separated
               automatically)

manifest.csv   file, PDF page, printed number, page position (600dpi px),
               and status (trusted / split / label-verified / etc.)
index.html     browser gallery of everything

Quality notes
-------------
Traced at 900 dpi rendering (~2.5x the scan's native ~360 ppi detail),
median-filtered to suppress scan ringing, thresholded per crop, and
upsampled 2x before potrace curve fitting — the outlines are smooth
bezier vectors that scale infinitely.

The scan's own resolution is the hard ceiling: there is no more detail
in the source than ~360 ppi, so these are as clean as the artwork allows.

Book quirks preserved
---------------------
- Number 1387 is printed out of position: it appears as a showcase mark
  on an earlier spread (page 72); the equivalent slot on page 74 carries
  an unnumbered mark (p74_03.svg).
- Numbers 677 and 1351 ARE present (they were fused into neighbouring
  marks and have been split back out).
- A handful of tall marks legitimately span two grid rows.
