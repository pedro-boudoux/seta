COOPER — "WORLD OF LOGOTYPES" — EXTRACTED LOGOTYPES
====================================================

Source: Alan Cooper, "World of Logotypes" (archive.org scan
compilation, 120 A4-landscape pages, embedded scans ~153 ppi).

Every logotype plate traced to a clean standalone SVG (black on
transparent, tight viewBox).

Naming: pNNN_XX = PDF page NNN, reading order XX (columns left to
right, top to bottom within a column) — the book's tiny caption
prints (~5 px tall in the source scan) are too small to OCR, so
entries are identified by position via manifest.csv.

Excluded from extraction (by design): the small caption/label lines,
alphabetical directory lists, address blocks, page watermarks
("Delivered by Logoblink.com" / "Scans by flickr.com/mr_carl").

Quality: the scan's ~153 ppi is the hard detail ceiling; pages were
rendered at 600 dpi (3.9x oversampled), median-despeckled,
per-crop Otsu thresholded, and 2x upsampled before potrace bezier
fitting. Outlines are smooth vectors that scale infinitely.

The -smooth sibling folder holds the same set traced with a gentle
Gaussian pre-blur (sigma 2.0 at 600 dpi, ~0.5% of a typical mark)
and rounder curve settings — same shapes, calmer edges.
